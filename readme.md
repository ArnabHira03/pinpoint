to run:

1. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
2. Click "Load unpacked" and choose ~/Desktop/Dev/markdrop_v3/extension
3. Open a website and click the Pinpoint icon (under the puzzle-piece menu).
4. Click the page, write a comment, then Save or press Ctrl+Enter.

Comments are listed in the panel on the right. Click one to jump to its pin, or use Edit and Delete.
Hide collapses the panel so you can pin what's underneath it.

Esc cancels the open comment box, then exits comment mode. The icon also toggles comment mode.

Comments are saved in this browser for each page. After a page reload, click the icon to bring them back.

Pins attach to the part of the page you clicked and follow it when the layout changes. An amber pin or a note in the panel means that part of the page changed, is hidden, or is gone.

After changing the code, click reload on the extension's card and reload the website.
