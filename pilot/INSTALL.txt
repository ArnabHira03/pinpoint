PINPOINT: HOW TO JOIN A REVIEW

Pinpoint lets you drop numbered comment pins on a live web page. Other people in the
review see your pins. It works in Chrome and Brave on a computer, not on a phone.


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)

1. Unzip the file. You get a folder called "pinpoint". On Windows, right-click the zip and
   choose "Extract All" first; just double-clicking it won't work. The "pinpoint" folder may
   be inside another folder with the zip's name.
2. Move that folder somewhere permanent, such as your Documents folder.

   IMPORTANT: leave the folder there and don't delete it. The browser runs Pinpoint from
   it. If the folder is moved or deleted, Pinpoint stops working. After loading it again
   you join as a new person and can't change your earlier comments.

3. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, or brave://extensions in Brave.
4. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
5. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "pinpoint" folder. If you see "Manifest file is
   missing", you chose the wrong folder: pick the one with manifest.json directly inside it.
6. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Pinpoint so it's easy to reach.

The browser may warn you about developer-mode extensions. That is expected, because
Pinpoint is shared directly and not through a web store.


JOIN THE REVIEW

1. Click the Pinpoint icon.
2. Enter your name. Others will see it on your comments, and you can't change it later.
3. Paste the share code you were sent into the "Share code" box and click Join.

The first request can take about a minute while the server wakes up. Please wait for it.


LEAVE A COMMENT

1. Open the page you were asked to review.
2. Click the Pinpoint icon, then "Comment on this page".
3. Click a spot on the page, type your comment, and save it.

After a page loads or reloads, click the icon and "Comment on this page" again to see the pins.

Good to know:
- Links and buttons on the page don't respond while commenting, so clicks can't open
  anything by accident. To use the page normally, click the icon and "Comment on this
  page" again to turn commenting off.
- The comment panel covers the right edge of the page. Click "Hide" to reach what's under it.
- New comments from other people appear within about 5 seconds while you're commenting.
- You can edit or delete your own comments, but not other people's.
- Pinpoint can't run on browser pages such as chrome://extensions, or on web store pages.


PRIVACY

These are saved on the review's server and can be seen by people in the review: your name,
your comments, the address of each page you comment on, and a short piece of text from
the part of the page you clicked. Please don't comment on pages showing private information.


IF YOU GET AN UPDATE

Replace the files inside the same "pinpoint" folder. Then open the extensions page and click
the reload icon on the Pinpoint card. Keep the folder in the same place.


WHEN YOU'VE FINISHED

Please tell the person who invited you:
1. What was confusing?
2. What broke or didn't work? Include the page address if you can.
3. What did you want to do but couldn't?
